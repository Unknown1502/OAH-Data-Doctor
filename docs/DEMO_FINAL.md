# Demo video: final film

The 3–5 minute demonstration video, made from the running app. This file is generated by the production pipeline
(`package.py`) from the same timeline that rendered the film, so it matches the video. Media files live outside the
repository, in `../artifacts/demo/`.

## Result

- **Video:** `artifacts/demo/demo-final.mp4`, 4:26.9 (266.95 s), 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC; sha256 `6ee159c6cfd3fa69…`.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, Daniel (ElevenLabs premade: British, middle-aged, formal, informative), voice id `onwK4e9ZLuTAKqWW03F9`; character timestamps drive the captions and the motion cues.
- **Captions:** 66 cues, at most 60 characters, burned in and in `demo-final.srt`.
- **Data:** snapshot `2026-09-30T09-08-55Z` (manifest sha256 `a80584b56487…`), language model off. The video says it is a snapshot and that the same finding was reproduced live.

| Key handling | read from the environment (`ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`), never printed, logged, written or committed |

## Browser route

1. **Data health** (`/`): the two verdicts (FHIR Pass, Data Doctor Critical) and the headline value.
2. **Finding** `F-SEM-SCALE-001-3d690239c32e` (`Observation/Obs-Almyros-TemperatureWater-2013`): the evidence chain (observed, calculation, result, rule broken, conclusion, root cause); the evidence table (FHIRPath) and provenance rows; **View raw resource** (drawer, flagged fields), Escape; *Where these values first appear* (IG spreadsheet, line 125); *What this affects* (trace), click the claim node.
3. **Compare** (`/compare`): the chip *Obesity … Female, 35-74 y vs Nordre Aker, 18-29 y*; verdict, matrix, *What you can do instead*; back to the top, the chip *Water temperature … Almyros 2013 vs 2014* (blocked).
4. **Check a claim** (`/claims`): *PM10 at Benevento site 04 exceeded the WHO guideline in 2018* (supported, reaches comparison); *PM2.5 causes cardiovascular disease in Benevento* (unsupported, reaches description; safe wording).
5. **Report** (`/report`): *Export FHIR OperationOutcome*; the executive finding's observed / derived / inferred / unknown rows; provenance; the sidebar's *Language model: off*.

## Captions

Caption timing comes from ElevenLabs character alignment, word by word. Spoken forms are shown in written form ("one hundred ninety-eight thousand degrees Celsius" → "198,000 °C", "Fire" → "FHIR", "ten to the fourth" → "10⁴"). Each spoken line is one caption or a few of balanced length, never ending on a small word; a caption moves to the top of the frame whenever it would cover the highlighted evidence. Emphasis colours only: passes (green), inconsistent and 198,000 (coral), 19.8, 10,000, 10⁴ and FHIR (cyan), unknown (amber).

Drawn in post-production over the real captures, never in the app, in the app's own palette (#071417, #E8F0F1, #67C7D9, #58D3A5, #E5B968, #FF766A):

- two cards with values read from the app: the published average and median, and the calculation as a fraction (198,000 over 19.8 = 10,000 = 10⁴ exactly), built from the backend's own operands and result;
- chapter labels; the "where Data Doctor sits" diagram (FHIR conformance below, evidence integrity above); the end card.

## Fact check

Before rendering, `storyboard.check_facts` compares every narrated number and verdict with what the app showed during the recording, and stops the render on any difference: the four published values; the calculation, its operands and "exactly 10⁴"; FHIR Pass and Data Doctor Critical; the rule text and "does not hold"; root cause UNKNOWN with the hypothesis marked not verified; the lineage (line 125, all values matching, before FHIR conversion); the impact counts; each comparability row named in the narration; the blocked Almyros comparison; both claim verdicts and ladders; the OperationOutcome export; the report's four statement kinds; the model shown as off; snapshot mode.

## Limitations

- The narration is synthetic (ElevenLabs); the voice was chosen on its documented character and measured pace, and a person should listen to the whole film once before upload (pronunciation of Almyros, FHIR, OperationOutcome).
- The cursor is drawn in post-production at the real click positions; the clicks themselves were made by the browser automation.
- The impact trace covers only the analyses Data Doctor computes, and the root cause stays unknown; the film says both.
- The end card names the repository URL, which works once the repository is pushed.
